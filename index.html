Bulk QR Code Generator for A4 Printing

This app allows you to paste bulk text or numbers, generate QR codes, arrange them on an A4 sheet, and print/download for cutting and pasting.

Features

  • Paste multiple values (one per line)
  • Generate QR codes in bulk
  • Arrange QR codes in printable A4 layout
  • Print directly from browser
  • Useful for labels, file tracking, court files, and stickers
